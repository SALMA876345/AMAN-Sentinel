# Retrieval Method Recommendation

**Recommendation: use Hybrid-RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion of BM25 + dense, k=60) as the retrieval method for escalated-claim evidence packs.**

On the held-out TEST split (500 sampled evaluable queries, fixed seed 42 -- see caveats below), Hybrid-RRF was the best or tied-best method on every cutoff except Recall@1: Recall@5 = 0.108 vs. 0.092 (hybrid-weighted / dense) and 0.076 (BM25); Recall@10 = 0.202 vs. 0.176 (hybrid-weighted) and 0.14 (BM25); MRR = 0.0787 vs. 0.0723 (hybrid-weighted), 0.0618 (BM25), and 0.0596 (dense). Only at Recall@1 does the existing weighted min-max hybrid edge it out narrowly (0.028 vs 0.022). This is a genuine, measured result, not an assumption: dense-alone actually beat BM25-alone on most cutoffs (multilingual claim/evidence pairs here favor semantic over lexical overlap), and both hybrid variants beat either single retriever, with RRF's rank-based fusion generalizing better than the min-max weighted score fusion at this corpus scale and score distribution. Given escalated claims are exactly the cases where the top-1 result is least likely to be sufficient on its own (that's why they were escalated), optimizing for Recall@5/@10 and MRR over strict Recall@1 is the right trade-off for a "deep verification" evidence pack that an LLM/human will read several passages from, not just the top hit -- which further favors Hybrid-RRF.

**Important caveat on what these numbers actually measure.** All four methods' numbers above come entirely from VERA-ARAB-sourced queries. Under the leakage-respecting constraint that the retrieval corpus may only contain TRAIN+VALIDATION evidence, we found a structural, data-driven fact: 0 of 169 AraStance and 0 of 638 ArCOV19(-tweets/-claims) TEST rows have their own gold evidence passage present anywhere in the TRAIN+VALIDATION corpus, because each of those claims is paired with an essentially unique, non-reused article. Only VERA-ARAB -- whose evidence was itself assigned by a prior TF-IDF retrieval step against a shared passage pool during Phase 1 restoration, i.e. "backfilled" rather than native ground truth -- has enough passage reuse (44.7% of its TEST rows) to produce any measurable Recall/MRR at all. So the ranking above is a fair comparison of the four methods against each other, but it is not validated against AraStance's genuine, independently-authored ground truth, and the absolute recall levels (mid-single-digit to low-double-digit percentages) partly reflect that VERA-ARAB's passage pool contains many lexically/topically similar near-duplicate passages, not just retrieval weakness. Before fully trusting this for production, spot-check Hybrid-RRF qualitatively on a sample of AraStance-style claims (unique-article provenance) since no quantitative signal could be computed for that regime here.
